You hatch in the reef shallows, barely longer than a grain of sand. Everything out there is bigger than you. Eat, grow, and choose what you become — then swim past the light, into the trench, and find the thing that waits at the bottom.

Staying alive down there

Swim with W and steer with the mouse — your fish goes where its nose points, so pitch up to rise and down to dive.

  • W swim forward
  • S slow / back up
  • AD turn
  • RF rise / dive
  • Mouse steer and pitch
  • Shift burst of speed
  • Space bite
  • E eat what's in front of you
  • V evolve when ready
  • Esc pause
Eat anything smaller than you. Touch it and it's gone. Anything roughly your size has to be bitten down first.
Red edges mean run. A creature four levels above you will kill you in seconds. Burst away and break its pursuit.
Depth is progress. Each ring of ocean holds stronger prey and better food. The abyss also unlocks evolution paths that the reef never will.
The Kraken is real. It sits in the trench past the abyss. Reach level 24 with a strong form before you go looking.

Evolution paths

Every form is a fork. Speed outruns what it can't fight, bulk survives what it can't outrun, and the abyssal line is only offered to a fish that evolves while deep in the dark.
